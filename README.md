# Palm92 Capital Intelligence

UK-first governed capital readiness, funding and wealth-risk intelligence.

**AI investigates. Humans decide. Evidence proves why.**

Palm92 Capital Intelligence is a decision-support platform for assessing capital readiness, comparing funding routes, modelling leverage, stress-testing downside risk, and preserving human control over consequential financial decisions.

## Core modules

- Document Intake
- Explainable Capital Readiness Score
- Credit Readiness
- Capital Options
- Capital Flywheel
- Wealth Risk Intelligence
- Governance and Human Approval
- Evidence and Provenance

## Product principle

> Never model the upside without modelling the failure case.

## Current MVP

The application now includes:

- privacy-safe demo document intake
- evidence register
- explainable 0–100 Palm92 Capital Readiness planning score
- factor-by-factor scoring for utilisation, debt-to-income, reserves, recent applications, payment conduct and evidence completeness
- prioritised readiness actions
- leverage and borrowing-cost modelling
- downside market stress testing
- stressed equity and loan-to-value
- explicit governance and professional-review boundaries

Live app: https://palm92-capital-intelligence.lovable.app

## Document intake boundary

The current GitHub MVP does not pretend to perform secure production PDF extraction. File selection is session-local and records file names for the demo workflow. Production use requires secure storage, encryption, access controls, retention rules, malware scanning, extraction provenance and privacy review before real financial documents are processed.

## Important boundary

Palm92 Capital Intelligence is decision support. The Palm92 Capital Readiness Score is an internal planning indicator, not a credit score, lender approval probability or recommendation to borrow. The platform does not provide regulated financial, investment, lending, legal or tax advice. It does not autonomously borrow, invest, submit credit applications, fabricate disputes, or guarantee lender outcomes.
