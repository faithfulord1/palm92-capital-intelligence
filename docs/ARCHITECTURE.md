# Architecture

## Flow

Data Collection
→ AI Intelligence Engine
→ Credit and Capital Risk Engines
→ Scenario and Stress Testing
→ Human Governance and Approval
→ Evidence and Audit Trail
→ Executive Dashboard

## Engines

### Credit Readiness
Assesses preparation factors before a user considers an application.

### Capital Options
Compares possible funding routes without pretending to know proprietary lender underwriting.

### Capital Flywheel
Models how capital may be protected, deployed, reinvested and compounded.

### Wealth Risk Intelligence
Models downside scenarios including asset falls, higher borrowing costs, cash-flow stress and collateral pressure.

### Governance
Requires human authority for consequential actions and preserves evidence explaining why a recommendation was produced.
