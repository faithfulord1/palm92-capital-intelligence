# Governance

## Core principle

AI investigates. Humans decide. Evidence proves why.

## Prohibited autonomous actions

The system must not autonomously:

- borrow money
- move funds
- purchase investments or assets
- submit credit or lending applications
- fabricate or mass-submit credit disputes
- claim certainty about lender approval
- present tax outcomes as guaranteed
- conceal material downside risk

## Human approval

Consequential actions require explicit human review.

## Evidence

Every material recommendation should retain:

- source data
- assumptions
- calculations
- risk flags
- model or rule version
- human decision
- timestamped audit record

## Risk presentation

Upside and downside scenarios should be presented together.

## Advisory boundary

The platform is decision support and should route regulated or specialist questions to appropriately qualified professionals where required.
